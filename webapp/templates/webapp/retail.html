Retail Data Quality (real public data)

A separate module that loads UCI Online Retail II: about 1.07 million real invoice lines from a UK online retailer (2009-12 to 2011-12, GBP). It shows how messy real data is cleaned without losing anything. This data has no Stripe or QuickBooks IDs, so it is never matched against the reconciliation, and Country is the customer's country, not a store location.
